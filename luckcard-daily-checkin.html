2. Participation Conditions

This campaign is available to registered Luckcard users.

3. Check-in Rules

  1. Users can receive one check-in reward per day. The 7-day rewards are 10, 10, 20, 20, 30, 40 and 50 Coins. From Day 8 onward, the daily reward remains 50 Coins while the streak continues.
  2. If the user misses a day, consecutive check-in days are recalculated from Day 1.
  3. Users can turn daily check-in reminders on or off from the Daily Bonus page.
  4. Coin earnings and usage records are available under Coin details.

4. Gold Coin Rebate Rules

1 Coin = ₦1. Gold coins can be automatically used for order deductions. The deduction on each order cannot exceed 0.5% of the order amount and is rounded down.

If an order fails, is cancelled or refunded, the deducted Coins are returned to the user account without changing the check-in record.
