Program Rules

1. Direct Invite Bonus

You earn a direct bonus (up to ₦5,000 based on VIP tier) when your invitee registers and completes a total order volume of 100 points within 30 days.

2. Passive Commissions

Earn a percentage of your network's trade volume (up to 4 layers deep, depending on your VIP tier). Passive commissions only trigger on orders greater than 100 points.

3. 20% Extra Bonus

If you generate 100 points or more in total referral earnings (Direct + Commission) within a single calendar month, a 20% extra bonus will be applied to those earnings at the end of the month.

4. Fraud Prevention

Self-referrals via same device ID or identical IPs are strictly prohibited and will result in account suspension and forfeiture of rewards.
