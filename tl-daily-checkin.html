1. Who can participate?

Daily check-in is available to registered TroveLoop users. Participation means you agree to the activity rules.

2. Daily check-in rewards

  1. Check in once per day. Each day runs from 00:00 to 23:59 WAT (UTC+1).
  2. Keep your streak going to earn more coins. From Day 8 onward, you earn 50 Coins each day while your streak continues.
  3. If you miss a day, your streak resets to Day 1. Missed days cannot be claimed later.

3. Using your coins

  1. Coins do not expire. Their Points value follows TroveLoop's conversion settings. Naira estimates use the current exchange rate.
  2. The amount of Coins you can use depends on TroveLoop's current order settings and your available balance.
  3. Turn on automatic use to apply eligible coins at checkout. When it is off, select coins manually when placing an order.
  4. Coin deductions, settlement and returns follow the rules of the related order. You can review the results in Treasure Log.

4. Reminders & records

Manage your daily reminder preference on the Daily Trove page. View earned and used coins in Treasure Log.

Coin rewards are for order deductions and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
